# Test Cases: Checkout Plate Tally

| Test Case ID | Title | Covers | Preconditions | Steps | Expected Result |
| --- | --- | --- | --- | --- | --- |
| TC-CheckoutTally-01 | Successful checkout tally | Basic Path | Guest's plates are commissioned with menu item, price tier, and "born on" timestamp; checkout RFID reader operational; price tiers mapped to plate colors. | 1. Guest signals readiness to pay. 2. Cashier directs guest to place plates on the checkout reader. 3. Cashier reviews the itemized tally and subtotal with the guest. 4. Guest confirms the total. 5. Cashier processes payment. | Live Inventory shows each tallied plate's RFID UID with a "sold" status, a sale timestamp, and the transaction ID; the guest's total equals the sum of the tallied price tiers. |
| TC-CheckoutTally-02 | Guest disputes the tally | Alternate Path A | Same as TC-CheckoutTally-01, through the Cashier reviewing the tally with the guest. | 1. Guest disputes the tally, citing a specific plate discrepancy. 2. Cashier inspects the disputed plate(s) and applies a manual override at the tally screen. 3. Cashier re-reviews the revised tally with the guest. 4. Guest confirms the revised total. | Live Inventory shows the Basic Path exit state, plus an override log entry naming the Cashier's ID and the disputed plate. |
| TC-CheckoutTally-03 | Checkout reader can't read every plate in the stack | Exception Path A | Guest's plate stack is on the checkout reader; the stack is dense enough that some tags aren't read on the first pass. | 1. Cashier enters the physical plate count. 2. Kaiten IQ flags a mismatch between the physical count and tags read. 3. Cashier rescans the stack in smaller batches. 4. Kaiten IQ confirms a full match. | Kaiten IQ proceeds to tally the subtotal (Basic Path #4) using the confirmed, fully-read plate set; no plate is billed or omitted based on the incomplete first read. |
| TC-CheckoutTally-04 | Guest presents a plate already flagged for removal | Exception Path B | A plate at the guest's table has a Live Inventory record already showing a "removed" status from [Automatic Freshness Pull](03b-automatic-freshness-pull.md) (e.g. TC-FreshnessPull-01's plate). | 1. Cashier scans the guest's plate stack, including the previously flagged plate. 2. Kaiten IQ flags the plate to the Cashier as previously flagged for removal. 3. Cashier inspects the plate and decides to decline the sale. 4. Cashier enters "decline" at the tally screen. | The plate's Live Inventory record shows a "declined at checkout" status, a resolution timestamp, and the Cashier's ID; it is excluded from the guest's tally and total. |
